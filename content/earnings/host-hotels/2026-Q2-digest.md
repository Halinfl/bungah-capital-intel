## Capital headline

Host delivered a **rate-led owner-operator beat**: comparable hotel RevPAR **+7.0%** to **$251.53** and Total RevPAR **+5.9%**, with leisure transient + group strength and a FIFA World Cup overlay. Management **raised FY 2026 comparable RevPAR and Total RevPAR growth to 4.75%–5.25%**. Capital story is recycling + ROI CapEx (Marriott/Hyatt transformational programs; Grand Hyatt Washington completed in Q2) plus a fortress IG balance sheet ($5.1B debt, **no 2026 maturities**, ~$3.6B liquidity) after a large special dividend funded by Four Seasons–related taxable gains. Wage pressure is acknowledged but **offset by rate** in the quarter — watch 2H margin guide for labor stickiness.
