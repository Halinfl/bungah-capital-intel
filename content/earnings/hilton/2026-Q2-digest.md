## Capital headline

Hilton’s Q2 print is a **fee-platform compounding** quarter, not an owned-RevPAR story: system-wide comparable RevPAR +3.9% currency-neutral with management + franchise fees +6.4%, Adjusted EBITDA $1.054B, and **record development pipeline** (541,300 rooms) with net unit growth running 6.1% trailing. Management is returning capital at scale (~$3.5B FY capital-return guide; $966M returned in Q2 incl. dividends) while terming out debt ($1.0B 5.500% notes due 2031). For Capital Graph: owners still fund bricks; Hilton monetizes brand density, conversions, and new lifestyle/midscale product (Undergraduate by Hilton). MEA RevPAR −29.5% is the clear geographic risk flag — do not extrapolate U.S. strength globally.

## Supply, pipeline, conversions, renovations / CapEx

- Opened **207 hotels / 24,100 rooms**; **21,600 net** rooms in Q2; openings **+50%** seq. vs Q1 (`company release`)  
- Approvals **42,900** rooms in Q2 (second-largest approvals quarter in company history per highlight PDF) (`slides` / `company release`)  
- Pipeline **3,853 hotels / 541,300 rooms** across 132 countries/territories as of 2026-06-30; **+6%** rooms vs YE prior-year compare; almost half under construction; **>50%** rooms outside U.S. (`company release`)  
- Trailing net unit growth **6.1%** (Jun 2025 → Jun 2026) for managed/franchised/strategic partner hotels (`slides` / `company release`)  
- New brand: **Undergraduate by Hilton** (upper-midscale / college-university markets); long-term growth potential cited as **>400 hotels** (`slides`)  
- Notable: Conrad Athens The Ilisian; first three **Apartment Collection by Hilton**; Waldorf Astoria Miami Beach signing (`company release`)  
- Q2 CapEx + contract acquisition costs: **$88M** (CapEx $37M + contract acquisition $51M net) (`company release`)  
- System size: **9,332 hotels / 1,363,441 rooms** (+ HGV timeshare system total 9,453 / 1,384,842) (`company release`)

**Atlas handoff:** pipeline conversion rate + Undergraduate / Apartment Collection absorption; conversion vs new-build mix.

## Segment / brand / geography mix

- **Fee engine dominates:** franchise/licensing $808M vs ownership revenue $311M in Q2 (`company release`)  
- Ownership segment RevPAR **−3.4%** (ADR −4.8%) vs management/franchise RevPAR **+4.0%** — owned book is not the growth story (`company release`)  
- Brand standouts (RevPAR y/y): Graduate +8.8%, Tapestry +6.6%, Home2 +5.9%, LXR +5.8%, Tru +5.1%, Embassy +5.2%; Waldorf Astoria −1.1%, Conrad flat (`company release`)
